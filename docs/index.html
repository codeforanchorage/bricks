THE BRICK PICK-UP HAS ENDED - THANK YOU, ANCHORAGE!

Anchorage Town Square Brick Search

A free volunteer tool from Code for Anchorage

The Town Square Park brick pick-up wrapped up on Saturday, October 10, 2026. Thank you to everyone who came out to bring home a piece of Anchorage history, and to the Parks & Recreation staff and volunteers who photographed, sorted and handed out thousands of engraved bricks over the past two months.

This search tool has been retired. Now that the pick-up is over, the remaining bricks are no longer individually tracked, so the search can no longer tell you where a particular brick is. The Parks & Recreation Flickr albums remain online as a photo record of the bricks on each pallet.

Read more about the bricks and the people who came for them: Anchorage Daily News, Aug. 11 · Sept. 30 update.

This page was a volunteer project of Code for Anchorage. From August to October 2026 it let brick owners look up more than 13,000 bricks by name or number, and see a photo of the 10,000-plus bricks that were matched to a pallet. The code is open source at github.com/codeforanchorage/bricks.
